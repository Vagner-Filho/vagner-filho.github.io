> ROLE: Senior Software Engineer

> STATUS: ACTIVE

> LOCATION: Brazil (UTC - 4)

> ME

As an engineer, I am always eager to experiment new ideas and get creative. So you will often see me avoiding Flat Design. Not because it is bad, but because solving the problems it does is difficult and provides an interesting challenge. On the server side of things, I try many approaches and techniques, from Rails and Node to Go and Rust. Each tool has its place and the curiosity to know them deeply allows me to focus on the work at hand instead of personal preferences over one or another.

I live with my fiance in the super cool pantanal biome. Even though we live in a capital city, we see many animals walking around and birds otherwise only seen in zoos. I have 3 dogs and a cat, I don't eat meat, I like to exercise and I care for the plants we have at home. On the weekends I am a volunteer at 2 projects: one converts book sales to dog shelters and the other provides social assistance, lectures and evangelization to a vulnerable community. In my spare time, besides toying with personal projects, I play video games, mostly on the PC through steam and have table top nights with family and friends. I also really like to ride and tweek motorcycles, so at this point I owned 4 and can't wait to get to the 5th :)

  • Backend (Node, Go)
